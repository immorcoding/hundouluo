# Asset provenance manifest

This manifest records the original sound assets prepared for issue #20 and the provenance of the existing original visual assets they are intended to accompany. This is a resource-preparation change only: it does not wire audio into scenes or establish complete issue acceptance.

## Generated audio

All files below are deterministic, mono 44.1 kHz, signed 16-bit PCM WAVs. They are generated by `tools/generate_sfx.py` from oscillator equations, pitch sweeps, harmonic layers, and shaped envelopes. The script uses only the Python standard library; it reads no source audio, sample pack, preset, external file, network service, or random input. Re-run `python tools/generate_sfx.py` from the repository root to recreate the files in `assets/audio/`.

| File | Intended cue | Approx. duration | Source and provenance |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `assets/audio/player_shot.wav` | Light, brief friendly shot; intentionally lower in level than danger cues. | 0.105 s | Generated from the original frequency-swept oscillator in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/enemy_shot.wav` | Short, lower-pitched enemy projectile cue. | 0.125 s | Generated from original oscillator and harmonic parameters in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/hit_confirm.wav` | Crisp metallic hit confirmation. | 0.132 s | Generated from original layered tones and a deterministic decay envelope in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/operative_hurt.wav` | More prominent descending damage cue for the operative. | 0.285 s | Generated from original swept oscillator and harmonic parameters in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/enemy_warning.wav` | Two-pulse rising warning cue for an enemy attack tell. | 0.320 s | Generated from original swept oscillator and shaped pulses in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/mech_charge_warning.wav` | Longer, swelling low mechanical warning for the defense mech charge. | 0.520 s | Generated from original layered oscillator, amplitude swell, and pulse modulation in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/death_health.wav` | Descending cue for health reaching zero. | 0.360 s | Generated from original swept oscillator and harmonic parameters in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |
| `assets/audio/death_fall.wav` | Airy fall followed by a low synthetic impact, distinct from health loss. | 0.390 s | Generated from original swept oscillator and a deterministic impact envelope in `tools/generate_sfx.py`. |

No third-party audio files, fonts, samples, or presets are included. There are no per-asset third-party attribution or redistribution terms to carry for these generated waveforms. The generator source is covered by the repository's `LICENSE`; this provenance note is not a legal opinion or a claim of exclusive rights in generated output.

Run the independent resource checks with:

```powershell
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_sfx_assets.py -v
```

The check exercises the generator command-line seam, verifies reproducibility for the light-shot cue, checks the generated cue set and WAV format/level constraints, and asserts that this manifest names the audio and visual provenance sources.

The audio assets have likewise been composed from scratch in code and do not reuse the sounds of existing games.

## #29 original combat art integration

`assets/combat_v011/atlas.png` is the sole newly referenced runtime bitmap: a 192×320 atlas containing the friendly, mechanical-soldier and defense-mech projectiles, both muzzle flashes, two impact effects and the charge ring. `assets/combat_v011/source.png`, `PROMPT.txt`, `build.gd`, `atlas.json` and `README.md` preserve the original ImageGen-assisted source, exact prompt, deterministic processing and frame contract from the approved #26 branch (`afc81bd`, art correction `3004d42`). No third-party pack was added. The source and exported atlas ship under this repository's MIT `LICENSE`. Review-only staged captures and the high-barrel-to-low-lane proposal were not imported into runtime assets; see [`art/issue-29-validation.md`](art/issue-29-validation.md).

## #33 approved entry gate integration

`assets/entry_gate_v011/gate.png` losslessly packs the eight approved #32 B-left16 PNG exports at 1:1. The production position is x3396; the x3388 range-gap candidate is excluded. Editable geometry, palette, deck-source crops and SVGs remain in `art/entry_gate/b-left/`, `art/entry_gate/ab/` and `art/entry_gate/build.py`. See [`assets/entry_gate_v011/README.md`](../assets/entry_gate_v011/README.md) for the frame/anchor contract and reconstruction command. Gate panels and frames are original program-drawn artwork under this repository's MIT license; wall/foundation textures reuse the original #22 `hangar_deck.png` material with provenance in `assets/art_source/README.md`. No new generated image or third-party pack was added.

## #30 approved UI and font distribution notices

The production HUD/outcome artwork in `assets/ui_v011/` reuses the user-approved #27 originals. Source regions, ImageGen provenance, native layout and reconstruction are recorded in [`assets/ui_v011/README.md`](../assets/ui_v011/README.md). No new image generation or third-party art pack was introduced.

The unmodified Fusion Pixel Font binary is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1, separately from this project's MIT license. Its source and exact SHA256 are recorded in that README. The Windows packaging tool copies this manifest into the playable archive; the following complete notices accompany the embedded font and its upstream sources. Original notice files also remain beside the production font.
