# ImageGen 提示词记录

使用内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，不是 CLI/API fallback。背景与平台 `transparent_background=false`；道具和三个角色 `true`。除背景第二次定向编辑输入本轮的背景首稿外，均为从文字生成，没有图像输入。以下为实际提交的提示词；生成模型未必严格满足其中尺寸、网格或像素画要求，最终尺寸与裁切以 `frames.json` 和装配脚本为准。

RIGHT THIRD: cathedral-sized cylindrical launch well and orbital rescue shuttle maintenance dock, cyan skylight from top right, multiple very distant curved galleries and deep vertical elevator shaft continuing BELOW the bottom of image. Dramatic controlled lighting: cool blue shafts from distant ceiling lights, dark steel mech silhouette against luminous desaturated blue atmosphere, very sparse tiny warm amber work lamps. Rich metal panel bevels, bolts, cables, pipes and wear but keep overall background atmospheric and mid contrast for bright foreground player legibility. Top edge framed by distant roof girders. CRITICAL: This is a BACKGROUND LAYER, NOT a complete gameplay scene: absolutely NO close foreground platforms, NO ground that a player stands on, NO player characters, NO small enemies, NO HUD, NO foreground crates or close posts, NO large near black pillars blocking the vista. Every structure is FAR AWAY. No text, no watermark, no logos. Original design. Palette navy / blue steel / cold cyan with restrained tiny amber. Fill whole canvas, no border.
